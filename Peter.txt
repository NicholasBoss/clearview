Here's a summary of everything that was changed to get NWS saving to the database:

ordersModel.js — Rewrote saveNWSData:

The old function was copied from Mirage 3500 and referenced wrong tables/variables. The new one follows the actual NWS schema:

Gets/inserts lookup values: frame_size, color, mesh, fastener, tab_spring
Creates a window record (junction linking all the above)
Creates a new_window_screen record (linked to window)
Creates an nws_measurement record (stores width/height fractions and +/- values)
Creates a customization record with nws_measurement_id
Creates an order record with the quantity
Creates order_customization with is_estimate=TRUE
Links the customer via cust_order and order_log
Also exported saveNWSData, confirmNWSOrder, and completeNWSOrder.

ordersController.js — Updated processNWSForm, added saveNWSOrder:

processNWSForm now saves to DB immediately (like Mirage 3500), stores customization_id in session, and handles Phone Order vs On-Site Order routing
saveNWSOrder (new) — called from the confirm page, sets is_confirmed=TRUE and redirects to account
ordersRoute.js — Added POST /saveNWS route pointing to saveNWSOrder

The flow now matches Mirage 3500:

Submit form → validates → processNWSForm saves to DB as estimate
Phone Order → straight to account page
On-Site Order → confirm page → "Confirm Order" → saveNWSOrder sets confirmed → account page
